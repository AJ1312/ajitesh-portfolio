The Concept

Tracking nutrition shouldn't require manually looking up every food item in a database. NutriVision Pro transforms food photography into instant, actionable nutritional intelligence. Point your camera at a plate, and multimodal AI identifies every item, estimates portion sizes, and delivers a complete macro and micronutrient breakdown in seconds.

Key Features

  • Instant Meal Photo Scanning: Real-time processing of food images.
  • Comprehensive Macro & Micronutrient Breakdown: Exact metrics derived from visual data.
  • Nutrition Label & Ingredient Inspector: OCR and structural extraction for packaged goods.
  • AI Nutritionist Insights: Contextual and personalized dietary recommendations based on scanned history.
  • Daily Meal History & Progress Tracking: Visual dashboard with progress rings and tracking bars.

Tech Details

  • Next.js App Router utilized for optimized serverless API routing.
  • Gemini Pro Vision multimodal prompting implemented with strict JSON schema enforcement to ensure deterministic output.
  • Vercel Edge functions deployed for low-latency global response times.
  • Framer Motion integrated for fluid micro-interactions across the UI.
  • LocalStorage leveraged for robust, client-side meal history persistence without mandatory backend auth overhead.

How It Works

  • 01
    Image Capture Upload or capture photo of meal or nutrition label directly from the interface.
  • 02
    Preprocessing Image is validated for quality, optimally resized, and securely encoded (Base64) for API transit.
  • 03
    Multimodal Vision Analysis Gemini Pro Vision processes the image alongside a highly structured engineering prompt.
  • 04
    Structured Extraction JSON schema enforced for precise extraction of items, portions, calories, and macros.
  • 05
    Validation Extracted data is cross-referenced against standardized nutritional heuristics to prevent hallucinations.
  • 06
    Results Data rendered to an interactive dashboard featuring animated progress rings, macro bars, and health scores.
