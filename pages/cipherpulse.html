The Incident

Modern enterprise networks carry over 80% encrypted traffic. Traditional deep packet inspection breaks down when payloads are wrapped in TLS 1.3. Security teams are blind to encrypted command-and-control channels, data exfiltration over HTTPS, and lateral movement disguised as legitimate cloud traffic. CipherPulse was built to see through the encryption without breaking it.

The Engineering

01

Multi-Threaded Producer-Consumer Pipeline

  • Consistent 5-tuple flow hashing (src_ip, dst_ip, src_port, dst_port, protocol)
  • Lock-free per-thread state eliminates shared-lock contention
  • Worker threads process flows independently with no mutex overhead
  • High throughput: packets distributed by flow hash, maintaining per-flow ordering
02

Post-SNI Encrypted Traffic Intelligence

  • JA4+ TLS fingerprinting captures cipher-suite and extension ordering
  • GREASE filtering removes randomized values that would pollute fingerprints
  • Encrypted flow classification without decryption
  • Fingerprint matching against published threat-intel databases
03

Real-Time Threat Scoring Engine

  • Welford's online algorithm for IAT (inter-arrival time) variance computation
  • Shannon payload entropy calculation for detecting encrypted payloads vs structured data
  • JA4+ IOC matching against Cobalt Strike, Sliver C2 fingerprints
  • C2 beaconing pattern detection in under 1.2ms per flow
