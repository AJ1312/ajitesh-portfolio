I am Ajitesh Sharma — a systems engineer and security researcher completing my B.Tech in Computer Science (Information Security) at VIT Vellore, investigating and building software engineered to hold under intense adversarial load. My focus centers on engineering projects with measurable real-world impact, conducting deep dives into systems security, and innovating where resilient architecture and machine learning intersect.

Real software craftsmanship isn't about layering ephemeral abstractions, but dissecting packet streams at the byte level, optimizing zero-copy ring buffers, and producing architectures that remain provably resilient in production environments.

This discipline has guided my work architecting three Indian patent applications in cognitive security and deepfake detection, publishing peer-reviewed research on proactive AI agent safety at IEEE COMPSAC, and deploying enterprise shift orchestration at Hindalco Industries (Aditya Birla Group).
