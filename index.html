EDITORIAL DISPATCH AUTHOR’S NOTE

Engineering at the Edges: From Packet Engines to Defensive AI

I investigate and build at the intersection of high-throughput systems, defensive cybersecurity, and streaming machine learning. I am drawn to problems where software must hold under pressure—whether that means dissecting JA4+ signatures from multi-gigabit packet streams without dropping frames, or training memory-bounded classifiers to catch rogue credentials in real time.

My approach is simple: diagnose from first principles, write lock-free resilient backends, and verify in production. This discipline has shaped three Indian patent applications in adaptive security and deepfake forensics, alongside peer-reviewed research accepted at IEEE COMPSAC.

The dispatches below document this work—the architectures engineered, the telemetry verified, and the defensive systems deployed.
